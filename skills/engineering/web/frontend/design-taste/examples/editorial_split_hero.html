Issue 04 / Spatial Systems

Interfaces shaped by typographic tension and calm restraint.

Tinted OKLCH surfaces, concentric container radii, and shared 1px hairlines replace heavy shadows and decorative gradient noise.

01 / CONTRAST

Stepped Lightness Over Drop Shadows

Separate elevated panes with a calibrated perceptual luminance delta and hairline perimeters instead of gray blur.

02 / NUMBERS

Tabular Alignment

Lock latency, pricing, and commit counters onto fixed-width numerals to eliminate horizontal jitter.

03 / TRACKING

Optical Headlines

Tighten display tracking between -0.025em and -0.04em so large serifs feel composed rather than loose.

04 / GEOMETRY

Concentric Radii

Keep nested controls concentric by subtracting inner padding from the parent radius.

05 / MOTION

Exponential Settling

Pair sub-180ms color shifts with tactile scale detents that respond immediately to pointer input.
